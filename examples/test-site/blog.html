Why we stopped taking notes in meetings

5 minute read

Taking notes in a meeting splits your attention between listening and writing. Most people end up doing both badly.

We recorded a month of our own calls and compared the notes we took with what was actually said. About a third of the decisions never made it into anyone's notes.

So we built Inkwell. It listens, then writes up decisions, owners and dates when the call ends.
